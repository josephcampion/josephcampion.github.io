While you’re here: this satellite is spinning about its intermediate axis, the one with the middle moment of inertia. Nothing pushes on it, yet every so often it flips end over end. That’s the tennis racket theorem (or Dzhanibekov effect), and it falls straight out of Euler’s equations for a rigid body. Click to spin it again.

Tumbling satellites are an old favorite of mine: see the EKF SLAM project on the School page.
