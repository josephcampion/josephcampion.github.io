Mini demos

Small interactive sims of the guidance and control ideas I use at work. They run in your browser.

  • Guidance · Beard & McLain ch. 10–11

    X-Wing Path Following

    Drop waypoints and the X-wing flies them with straight lines, fillets or Dubins paths, using vector-field path following.

  • Orbits · Attitude control

    Death Star Orbit & Attitude

    Click to pick a new orbit and the Death Star flies a two-burn Hohmann transfer, turning with a reaction wheel to aim its engine before each burn.

  • Robotics · Manipulator control

    Droid Arm Reach

    Click and a droid’s arm plans a smooth straight-line reach, solving inverse kinematics on the fly and using computed-torque control to follow it.

  • Propulsion · MIT 16.50

    Millennium Falcon: Turbojet to Ramjet

    Click to set a target Mach and the freighter climbs on an afterburning turbojet, then swings an inlet door to hand its air to a ramjet between Mach 2 and 3.
