Mini demos

Small interactive sims of the guidance and control ideas I use at work. They run in your browser.

  • Guidance · Beard & McLain ch. 10–11

    X-Wing Path Following

    Drop waypoints and watch an X-wing work out how to fly through them when it can’t turn on a dime.

  • Orbits · Attitude control

    Death Star Orbit & Attitude

    Pick a new orbit and the Death Star burns twice to get there, speeding up both times and ending up slower.

  • Robotics · Manipulator control

    Droid Arm Reach

    Click anywhere and a droid reaches for it in a smooth, straight line, a flat cousin of the arms I worked on at RE2.

  • Propulsion · Self-study

    Millennium Falcon: Turbojet to Ramjet

    Set a target Mach and watch the freighter hand off from a turbojet to a ramjet as it speeds up.
