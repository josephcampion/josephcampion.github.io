Senior GNC Engineer · Tactical Recon & Strike · Mar 2026–now · Atlanta

I work on Altius, a fixed-wing, tube-launched drone built for land, sea and air. So far I’ve worked on the autopilot, the target state estimator, and system identification of the flight dynamics & sensor models for the 6DOF simulation.

Hermeus

GNC Engineer → Lead GNC Engineer · Jun 2022–Mar 2026 · Atlanta

At Hermeus I worked on Quarterhorse, an F-16-sized drone designed to reach Mach 2.5 and beyond. I developed the flight controls and navigation for the supersonic Mk 2.1, built its 6DOF simulation in Julia, and designed propulsion controls that reached Mach 3 conditions at the Notre Dame Turbomachinery Lab.

Astrobotic Technology

GNC Engineer II · Nov 2021–May 2022 · Pittsburgh

At Astrobotic I worked primarily on Peregrine, a lunar lander that launched on ULA’s Vulcan Centaur. I wrote the sensor interfaces for a navigation flight test and analyzed Monte Carlo landing simulations.

RE2 Robotics

Controls Engineer II & III · Sep 2019–Oct 2021 · Pittsburgh

At RE2 (pronounced “are-ee-squared”) I did controls for robotic arms built for everywhere except the factory floor: aviation, energy, medicine, and defense. That meant controllers, trajectory planners, and visual servoing for 6- and 7-DOF arms.
