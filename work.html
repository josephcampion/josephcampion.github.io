Astrobotic Technology

Before Hermeus, I was a GNC Engineer at Astrobotic.

GNC stands for Guidance, Navigation, and Control. In other words, where is our spacecraft (N)? Where do we want it to go (G)? And how do we get from here to there (C)?

In this case, the destination is simply…to the Moon!
