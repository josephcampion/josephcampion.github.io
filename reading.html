Bookshelf

Reading list.

The textbooks behind the simulations and demos on this site.

Flight dynamics & control

  • Aircraft Control and Simulation Stevens, Lewis & Johnson · 3rd ed., 2015

    F-16 aero and engine tables, trim, and linear analysis for the F-16 6DOF sim.

  • Small Unmanned Aircraft: Theory and Practice Beard & McLain · 2012

    Autopilot loops, path following, fillets, and Dubins paths in the X-wing demo.

  • Dynamics of Flight: Stability and Control Etkin & Reid · 3rd ed., 1996

    Swept-wing aerodynamic center shift and damping-derivative Mach scaling.

  • Automatic Control of Aircraft and Missiles Blakelock · 2nd ed., 1991

    How damping falls off at high Mach.

Guidance & navigation

  • Tactical and Strategic Missile Guidance Zarchan · 6th ed., 2012

    Proportional navigation laws and bearing-only target tracking.

  • Probabilistic Robotics Thrun, Burgard & Fox · 2005

    EKF-SLAM prediction and update steps.

Aerodynamics & propulsion

  • Elements of Propulsion: Gas Turbines and Rockets Mattingly · 2006

    Turbojet and ramjet cycles for the freighter demo.

  • Modern Compressible Flow Anderson

    Prandtl–Glauert and Ackeret corrections for supersonic aero.

  • Aircraft Design: A Conceptual Approach Raymer · 6th ed., 2018

    Transonic drag rise and ground effect.

  • Flight Vehicle Aerodynamics Drela · 2014

    Blending models across the transonic region.

Spacecraft

  • Fundamentals of Spacecraft Attitude Determination and Control Markley & Crassidis · 2014

    Quaternion error feedback.

  • Space Vehicle Dynamics and Control Wie · 2nd ed., 2008

    Reaction wheels and momentum dumping in the Death Star demo.

  • Orbital Mechanics for Engineering Students Curtis

    Two-body orbits and Hohmann transfers.

Robotics

  • Robotics: Modelling, Planning and Control Siciliano, Sciavicco, Villani & Oriolo · 2009

    Redundant-arm inverse kinematics for the droid arm demo.

  • Robot Modeling and Control Spong, Hutchinson & Vidyasagar

    Computed-torque control.
